<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For <a href="https://joybird.com/">Joybird</a>, a modern furniture brand and subsidiary of La-Z-Boy Incorporated known for customizable designs and a digital-first customer experience, growth has always depended on understanding its customers deeply and meeting them at exactly the right moment.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">But as the brand scaled, so did the complexity of its marketing. Disconnected channels, manual processes, and limited visibility into performance made it harder to deliver the kind of personalized, high-impact experiences Joybird is known for.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That’s where AI came in.</span></p>

As the brand scaled, her team needed more advanced tools to execute on those goals efficiently.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">By adopting Iterable’s AI-powered capabilities, Joybird transformed how it engages customers—bringing together data, automation, and experimentation to drive measurable gains in engagement and revenue. The results earned them recognition at the <a href="https://iterable.com/blog/2025-iterable-expie-awards-winners/">2025 Iterable Expie Awards</a>, where they took home <a href="https://iterable.com/blog/2025-iterable-expie-awards-winners/"><b>Best AI-Powered Customer Experience</b></a></span><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>

<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">From Fragmented Insights to a Unified Strategy</span></h3>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Before switching to Iterable, <a href="https://iterable.com/customers/joybird/">Joybird</a> faced several challenges that hindered its marketing performance. The team struggled with disconnected data streams across their email and<a href="https://iterable.com/whitepaper/savvy-marketers-guide-to-building-a-stellar-sms-strategy/"> SMS marketing channels</a>, resulting in an incomplete view of customer engagement and limiting their ability to create cohesive campaigns. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The marketing team recognized the need to move away from manual segmentation to improve efficiency. They needed more than incremental improvements—they needed a <a href="https://iterable.com/features/centralized-data/">unified platform</a> that could offer an omnichannel AI-powered solution, provide a clearer picture of performance metrics, and enable them to scale their marketing efforts efficiently.</span></p>
<blockquote>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">&#8220;Since consolidating SMS and email using Iterable, we can confidently see what&#8217;s working and what&#8217;s not, and establish the right balance of SMS versus email messaging cadence for each user. Iterable SMS, paired with Looker insights, gives us specific clarity on SMS revenue attribution.&#8221;</p>
<p></span></p>
<p><b><i>~ Yvette Rojas, Email Marketing Manager at Joybird</i></b></p>
</blockquote>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Through this integration, Joybird has transformed fragmented insights into a well-rounded understanding of customer behavior, improving how they measure and refine their marketing efforts.</span></p>

<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">1. Timing Messages Intelligently with Send Time Optimization (STO)</span></h4>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><a href="https://iterable.com/blog/what-is-send-time-optimization/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Send Time Optimization</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (STO) is an Iterable AI feature that helps you send notifications when your contacts are most likely to engage. For each campaign recipient, STO analyzes historical engagement behavior and selects an optimal, per-person send time.</span></p>

<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">2. Prioritizing High-Intent Audiences with Brand Affinity</span></h4>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><a href="https://iterable.com/blog/introducing-brand-affinity/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Brand Affinity</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is an intelligent personalization solution powered by Iterable AI that gives marketers the power to transform customer engagement into more meaningful cross-channel experiences. It takes a holistic look at engagement across all channels and calculates a score that indicates how likely each contact will interact with future marketing messages. Based on their engagement, each contact is labeled (such as “loyal,” “positive,” “neutral,” or “negative”).</span></p>

<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">3. Re-Engaging Customers with Next Best Action</span></h4>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><a href="https://iterable.com/blog/exploring-iterables-next-best-action/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Next Best Action</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is an AI-driven feature that helps identify and re-engage previously engaged customers by helping to craft and automatically send the next best version of a previously completed campaign. It determines the optimal action to take with them at any given point in their journey by analyzing customer behavior and predicting the most effective way to drive engagement or conversion.</span></p>

<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">4. Eliminating Guesswork with Channel Optimization</span></h4>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><a href="https://support.iterable.com/hc/en-us/articles/13102589168916-Channel-Optimization"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Channel Optimization</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> analyzes a user&#8217;s historical data and sends messages to the channel they&#8217;re most likely to engage with. This eliminates the guesswork of which channel to use. The data is analyzed weekly to account for changes in channel preference. Channel Optimization ensures that users are alerted to time-sensitive messages on the channel where they have the highest probability of seeing them.</span></p>
