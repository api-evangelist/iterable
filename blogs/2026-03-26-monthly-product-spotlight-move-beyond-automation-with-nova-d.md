<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Marketing today requires more decisions than ever across channels, timing, and message frequency. But too often, those decisions come down to guesswork.</span></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nova Decisioning is Iterable’s AI-powered decisioning layer that helps eliminate the guesswork. It brings intelligent, data-driven decisions directly into marketers workflows, so they can make more data-driven decisions about how, when, and where to engage each customer. By learning from real customer behavior and engagement patterns, Nova Decisioning automatically adapts campaigns to reach people when they’re most likely to respond.&nbsp;</span></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This transforms how teams work &#8211; from a series of educated guesses to a simpler, more effective way of working. For marketers, it means fewer manual decisions, less complexity, and more consistent, relevant customer experiences as programs scale.</span></p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-channel-decisioning"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Channel Decisioning</span> </h3>



<p><b>Reach customers on the channel they’re most likely to engage with.</b></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A notification sent through the wrong channel might never be seen—undermining engagement when timing matters most.</span></p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-channel-decisioning"><span style="font-weight: 400;">What is Channel Decisioning?</span></h4>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Marketers often have to guess which channel will drive engagement. Instead of manually selecting email, push, or SMS for every campaign, Channel Decisioning predicts the channel each customer is most likely to respond to and automatically delivers messages there.</span></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For time-sensitive moments, like a back-in-stock alert for a limited item,this helps ensure messages are seen and acted on before it’s too late.</span></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image"><img alt="" class="wp-image-142845" src="https://iterable.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Channel-Decisioning-1.png" /></figure>



<p></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-send-time-decisioning"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Send Time Decisioning</span></h3>



<p><b>Send messages at the moment customers are most likely to engage.</b></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Many marketers rely on generalized “best send times,” even though engagement patterns vary widely across individuals.</span></p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-send-time-decisioning"><span style="font-weight: 400;">What is Send Time Decisioning?</span></h4>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">S</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">end Time Decisioning removes the guesswork by analyzing past engagement to determine when each customer is most likely to respond.&nbsp;</span></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This means messages are automatically sent at individualized times for every recipient, helping improve open rates and engagement without adding operational complexity.</span></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image"><img alt="" class="wp-image-142863" src="https://iterable.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Send-Time-Decisioning-1.png" /></figure>



<p></p>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-frequency-decisioning"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Frequency Decisioning</span></h3>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<p><b>Personalize how often to engage each customer.</b></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Finding the right messaging cadence is one of lifecycle marketing’s biggest challenges. Too many messages lead to fatigue and unsubscribes, while too few can mean missed engagement opportunities.</span></p>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-frequency-decisioning"><span style="font-weight: 400;">What is Frequency Decisioning?</span></h4>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Frequency Decisioning determines the ideal messaging cadence for each customer within the engagement limits you define.</span></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Instead of applying a static cap across your entire audience, you can set a range,such as 1–5 messages per week,and let AI determine the right cadence for each individual, maximizing engagement while minimizing unsubscribe risk.</span></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image"><img alt="" class="wp-image-142857" src="https://iterable.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Frequency-Decisioning.png" /></figure>



<p></p>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-smarter-engagement-with-nova-decisioning"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Smarter Engagement with Nova Decisioning</span></h3>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Together, Channel Decisioning, Send Time Decisioning, and Frequency Decisioning form the foundation of Nova Decisioning—optimizing three of the most important engagement decisions:</span></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><b>Where</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to reach customers</span></li>



<li><b>When</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to send messages</span></li>



<li><b>How often</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to engage</span></li>
</ul>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">By unifying these decisions into a single, intelligent framework, marketers can reduce complexity, scale personalization, and deliver more relevant, balanced customer experiences.</span></p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-faq"><span style="font-weight: 400;">FAQ</span></h3>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<p><strong>1. What is Nova Decisioning in Iterable?</strong></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nova Decisioning is Iterable’s AI-powered decisioning layer that helps marketers determine how, when, and where to engage each customer. By analyzing historical engagement patterns and behavioral signals, it automatically optimizes channel selection, send timing, and messaging cadence within campaign workflows.</span></p>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<p><strong>2. How is Nova Decisioning different from traditional automation?</strong></p>



<p><b></b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Traditional automation relies on predefined rules and fixed schedules. Nova Decisioning adapts in real time based on customer behavior, helping marketers move beyond static workflows to more personalized, data-driven engagement.</span></p>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<p><b>3. What’s the difference between Channel, Send Time, and Frequency Decisioning?</b></p>



<p>Each capability focuses on a different aspect of engagement:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><b>Channel Decisioning</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> selects the best channel (email, SMS, push) for each user</span></li>



<li><b>Send Time Decisioning</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> determines when a message is most likely to be opened</span></li>



<li><b>Frequency Decisioning</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> personalizes how often each user receives messages</span></li>
</ul>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Together, they form Nova Decisioning and optimize the most critical engagement decisions.</span></p>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<p><b>4. What types of use cases benefit most from Nova Decisioning?</b></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nova Decisioning is especially effective for:</span></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Time-sensitive campaigns (like back-in-stock alerts)</span></li>



<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">High-volume sends where engagement varies by user</span></li>



<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Programs where balancing frequency and avoiding fatigue is critical</span></li>
</ul>



<p>&nbsp;</p>



<p><b>5. Are Nova Decisioning features available to all plans?</b></p>



<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Yes. Channel and Send Time Decisioning are included in all plans. Frequency Decisioning is available to Premium AI Suite users.</span></p>
