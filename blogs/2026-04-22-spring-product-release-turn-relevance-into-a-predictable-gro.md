<p><strong>Marketing has changed. Most systems haven’t.</strong></p>



<p></p>



<p>Marketing is more competitive than ever. Attention is harder to earn. Customers move quickly, and AI-driven feeds decide what gets seen and what gets ignored. Even strong ideas fail if they can’t act on the moment they’re created for.</p>



<p>For many teams, the challenge is not creativity. It is execution at scale.</p>



<p>As programs grow, personalization becomes harder to maintain. Timing slips. Messages lose specificity. Teams compensate by doing more: more campaigns, more segments, more manual work. The result is more effort with less to show for it.&nbsp;</p>



<p><strong>This Spring, we’re introducing a major evolution of Iterable designed to close that gap.</strong></p>



<p></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-a-new-way-to-power-relevance-with-ai">A New Way to Power Relevance with AI</h3>



<p>Iterable’s Spring Release is built around a simple idea: relevance should scale as your program grows, not break under it.</p>



<p>We’re introducing an AI-powered system that brings execution, audience activation, and governance into a single, connected workflow. Instead of stitching together tools and processes, marketers become strategists, directing campaign vision and decision-making, while intelligent agents handle the heavy lifting across execution, optimization, and orchestration.</p>



<p>This release isn’t a single feature or workflow. It changes how marketing performs as you scale. You’ll see the impact across five areas:</p>



<p></p>



<p></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-1-scalable-personalization-powered-by-nova-agents">1. Scalable Personalization Powered by Nova Agents</h3>



<p>As programs scale, personalization is often the first thing to break. Segments become harder to maintain, timing becomes inconsistent, and campaigns rely more on manual setup than real customer behavior.</p>



<p><strong>Nova Agents</strong> take a different approach. Instead of manually defining every decision, teams set the goal—and the system handles how personalization is applied and improved over time.</p>



<p></p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-are-nova-agents-in-iterable"><strong>What are Nova Agents in Iterable?</strong></h4>



<p>Nova Agents are a coordinated system of goal-driven AI agents that turn customer behavior into action and marketing intent into execution.</p>



<p>Using natural language, marketers can define what they want to achieve—like increasing activation or driving repeat engagement. From there, agents continuously interpret customer behavior and optimize how campaigns perform against that goal—automating QA, testing variations, and surfacing insights so teams can improve results without added manual work.</p>



<p>These Nova Agents handle the execution work behind the scenes:</p>



<p></p>



<p class="has-text-align-center"><strong>Review Agent:</strong> ensures quality and accuracy across messages before they go live.</p>

<p class="has-text-align-center"><strong>Handlebars Agent:</strong> generates and applies dynamic personalization logic without requiring code.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-2-centralized-performance-insights-with-command-center">2. Centralized Performance Insights with Command Center</h3>



<p>Understanding performance shouldn’t require stitching together reports or digging through dashboards. Teams need a clear view of what’s driving results, how campaigns are contributing to goals, and where to focus next.</p>



<p></p>



<p class="has-text-align-center"><strong>Iterable Command Center</strong> brings that visibility into one place, so performance is always tied back to what you’re trying to achieve.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-the-iterable-command-center"><strong>What is the Iterable Command Center?</strong></h4>



<p>The Iterable Command Center is a centralized, goal-based view of performance across your marketing programs.</p>



<p>Instead of analyzing campaigns in isolation, teams can see how results connect to broader objectives—tracking progress, identifying what’s working, and spotting issues as they emerge.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-3-audience-intelligence-harnessed-in-real-time">3. Audience Intelligence Harnessed in Real Time</h3>



<p>Most teams can see customer behavior—but acting on it, especially across channels, is where things break down. Signals live in one place, activation happens in another, and by the time teams respond, the moment has passed.</p>



<p>Iterable connects audience insight directly to execution, so teams can act on intent as it happens.</p>



<p></p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-unknown-user-activation-in-iterable"><strong>What is Unknown User Activation in Iterable?</strong></h4>



<p><strong>Unknown User Activation</strong> allows teams to engage high-intent visitors before they identify themselves. Marketers define the signals that indicate readiness—like repeated visits or product views—and activate users only when that intent is clear.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter is-resized"><img alt="" class="wp-image-141531" height="810" src="https://iterable.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Unknown-User-Activation-3.png" style="width: 625px;" width="1080" /></figure>



<p></p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-google-ads-integration-in-iterable"><strong>What is Google Ads Integration in Iterable?</strong></h4>



<p><strong>Google Ads Integration</strong> keeps paid and owned audiences in sync automatically. Audiences built in Iterable can be sent directly to Google Ads in real time, so targeting, retargeting, and suppression always reflect current customer behavior.</p>



<p></p>



<p>Together, these capabilities enable teams to:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Engage high-intent visitors before they identify themselves</li>



<li>Sync audiences directly to paid channels in real time</li>



<li>Reduce wasted spend by suppressing the wrong audiences</li>



<li>Keep targeting aligned across owned and paid channels</li>
</ul>



<p>You get more value from the traffic you already have. Engagement starts earlier. Conversion improves without increasing spend.</p>



<p></p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-4-built-in-trust-compliance-and-control">4. Built-in Trust, Compliance, and Control</h3>



<p>Compliance, deliverability, and customer trust cannot be afterthoughts. Managing them across channels like SMS often requires manual checks across different tools, which slows teams down and increases risk.</p>



<p>Iterable builds governance directly into execution, so compliance becomes part of how campaigns run—not something handled after the fact.</p>



<p></p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-the-sms-compliance-toolkit-in-iterable"><strong>What is the SMS Compliance Toolkit in Iterable?</strong></h4>



<p><strong>The SMS Compliance Toolkit </strong>helps teams stay aligned with regulations by enforcing safeguards within messaging workflows. It provides visibility into consent, identifies high-risk recipients, and applies controls to prevent issues before messages are sent.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter is-resized"><img alt="" class="wp-image-141534" height="810" src="https://iterable.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/SMS-Compliance-Toolkit-1.png" style="width: 625px;" width="1080" /></figure>



<p></p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-are-stored-messages-in-iterable"><strong>What are Stored Messages in Iterable?</strong></h4>



<p><strong>Stored Messages</strong> provides secure, audit-ready records of customer communications. Teams can automatically archive rendered message copies in their own environment, making it easy to access and retrieve records when needed.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter is-resized"><img alt="" class="wp-image-141506" height="810" src="https://iterable.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/STORED-MESSAGES.png" style="width: 625px;" width="1080" /></figure>



<p></p>



<p>Together, these capabilities enable teams to:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Enforce compliance requirements automatically across SMS programs</li>



<li>Reduce legal and deliverability risk without manual intervention</li>



<li>Maintain clear, audit-ready records of customer communications</li>



<li>Move faster with confidence in how messages are sent and stored</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-faqs">FAQs</h3>



<p><strong>1. How does Iterable personalize customer experiences at scale?</strong></p>



<p>Iterable personalizes customer experiences by using goal-driven AI agents to continuously adapt campaigns based on real-time behavior and business objectives. With <strong>Nova Agent</strong>, teams can automate how messages are timed, targeted, and optimized, so personalization stays relevant as programs grow.</p>



<p><strong>2. How does Iterable activate audience data across channels?</strong></p>



<p>Iterable activates audience data by connecting customer behavior directly to execution across owned and paid channels. With <strong>Unknown User Activation</strong> and <strong>Google Ads integration</strong>, teams can engage high-intent users in real time and keep audiences synced, so targeting and messaging stay aligned across every touchpoint.</p>



<p><strong>3. How does Iterable help marketers measure campaign performance and ROI?</strong></p>



<p>Iterable helps marketers measure campaign performance and ROI through a centralized, goal-based view of results. With <strong>Iterable Command Center</strong>, teams can track progress across campaigns, identify what’s driving outcomes, and prioritize optimizations, without relying on fragmented dashboards or manual reporting.</p>
