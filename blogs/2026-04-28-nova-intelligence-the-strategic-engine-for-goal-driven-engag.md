<p>AI has reset expectations for how fast marketing should move—but execution hasn’t kept up. Behind every campaign, marketers are still managing thousands of decisions across manual workflows, status rules, and disconnected tools. As expectations rise, this gap is what slows growth.</p>



<p><strong>Nova Intelligence </strong>closes that gap. Nova acts as a reasoning partner, embedding AI into execution, insights, and decisioning. It turns real-time customer signals into immediate, personalized actions across every channel and interaction. By helping teams move from manual setup to goal-driven execution, Nova Intelligence connects strategy to action and makes it easier to scale marketing without added complexity. The result is more relevant experiences, greater efficiency, and stronger, more consistent growth.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-nova-intelligence">What Is Nova Intelligence?</h3>



<p>Nova Intelligence is not a single feature, but a connected ecosystem of goal-driven, context-aware Agents, Insights, and Decisioning. It continuously learns from real-time customer behavior and business context to eliminate busywork and optimize toward your specific outcomes.</p>



<p>Instead of marketers managing every microstep of a journey manually, they direct the strategy in plain language while Nova Intelligence handles the heavy lifting of building, personalizing, and auditing campaigns.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-does-nova-intelligence-actually-change-for-marketers">What Does Nova Intelligence Actually Change For Marketers?</h3>



<p>Nova Intelligence changes how marketing work gets done by connecting execution, optimization, and decisioning into a system that improves as it runs.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-1-personalization-without-the-complexity">1. Personalization Without the Complexity</h4>



<p>Personalization often breaks when it depends on manual rules and static segments. Nova Intelligence applies 1:1 logic across every channel—using real-time insights from customer behavior and business context to continuously adjust messages, timing, and targeting, so experiences stay relevant without added complexity.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-2-continuous-in-flight-optimization">2. Continuous, In-Flight Optimization</h4>



<p>Nova Intelligence doesn&#8217;t just run campaigns; it improves them while they’re live. By coordinating testing and applying real-time and predictive insights, it helps teams understand what’s working, anticipate what will work next, and continuously improve performance while campaigns are in flight.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-3-focus-on-strategy-not-safeguards">3. Focus on Strategy, Not Safeguards</h4>



<p>Execution tasks like message QA, complex logic, and compliance checks can slow teams down and introduce risk. Nova Intelligence automates these processes while maintaining audit-ready records, freeing marketers to focus on strategy, creative direction, and higher-value decisions rather than managing safeguards manually.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-4-faster-time-to-value">4. Faster Time-to-Value</h4>



<p>Nova Intelligence reduces the time between a campaign idea and reality. By letting marketers define goals in plain language, it removes engineering dependencies and technical bottlenecks—so teams can move from concept to execution quickly and act on customer intent the moment it happens.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-to-get-started-with-nova-intelligence">How to Get Started with Nova Intelligence</h3>



<p>Nova Intelligence turns strategy into execution by handling the steps that typically slow teams down—from setup to optimization.</p>



<p></p>



<p><strong>1. Define a Strategy:</strong> Ask a Nova Agent to build a re-engagement journey for inactive users.</p>



<p><strong>2. Automate the Build: </strong>Ask Nova to generate the segments, dynamic content, and A/B test variants instantly.</p>



<p><strong>3. Ensure Quality: </strong>Ask Nova to audit the journey to catch costly mistakes before they reach the customer.</p>



<p><strong>4. Optimize Results:</strong> Ask Nova to surface performance alerts, allowing the team to course-correct in real-time.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-frequently-asked-questions-about-nova-intelligence">Frequently Asked Questions About Nova Intelligence</h3>



<p id="h-1-do-marketers-still-have-control-over-the-ai-in-nova-intelligence"><strong>1. Do marketers still have control over the AI in Nova Intelligence?</strong></p>



<p>Absolutely. Marketers define the goals, strategy, and guardrails, while Nova Intelligence handles execution within those parameters—ensuring every action aligns with your intent.</p>



<p></p>



<p id="h-2-how-does-nova-intelligence-ensure-data-security-and-compliance"><strong>2. How does Nova Intelligence ensure data security and compliance?</strong></p>



<p>Nova Intelligence uses built-in permissioning and the MCP Server to provide AI-governed access to real customer data. This ensures every action is secure, compliant, and aligned with enterprise policies, so teams can confidently use AI within controlled, auditable boundaries.</p>



<p></p>



<p id="h-3-is-nova-intelligence-available-to-all-customers"><strong>3. Is Nova Intelligence available to all customers?</strong></p>



<p>Yes. The core Nova Intelligence layer is included for all customers, with additional agents and advanced decisioning capabilities available as add-ons.</p>



<p></p>



<p id="h-4-how-is-nova-intelligence-different-from-generative-ai-tools"><strong>4. How is Nova Intelligence different from generative AI tools?</strong></p>



<p>Unlike standalone generative AI tools, Nova Intelligence is connected directly to your campaign data and customer context. It doesn’t just generate content. It takes action, executing and optimizing campaigns based on real-time signals and defined goals.</p>
