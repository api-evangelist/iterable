Babylist scaled from 5 million to 1 billion annual emails while expanding to eight CRM channels, adopting agentic AI workflows powered by Claude to automate production tasks and cut email creation from roughly an hour to about 15 minutes. They also implemented automated QA processes that flag broken links and personalization issues within seconds, targeting a 50% reduction in email production time.
