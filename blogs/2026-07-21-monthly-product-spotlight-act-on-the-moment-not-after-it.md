Marketing moves fast, and customers expect every interaction to reflect what’s happening right now. But too often, marketers are blocked by disconnected systems and manual execution. Instead of responding to live customer and business signals, they’re forced to make decisions using yesterday’s information while customers continue to act in real-time.
